Divide and Conquer Algorithms (from your outline + common ones)

1. Merge Sort → splits array/list in half recursively, merges back
2. Quick Sort → splits around a pivot recursively, concatenates
3. Quickselect → pivot-based selection of k-th element
4. Binary Search → halves the search space each time

💡 Metaphor 🧠

Divide and conquer = keep cutting your task in half, solve small pieces, then combine
