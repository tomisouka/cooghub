Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors

Finding Eigenvalues
1. Solve det(A − λI) = 0
   → characteristic polynomial
2. Roots λ₁, λ₂, ... are eigenvalues
3. For each λ: solve (A−λI)v = 0
   → eigenvectors span eigenspace Eλ
Propertiestr(A) = Σλᵢ    det(A) = Πλᵢ
Diagonalization
ConceptMeaning
