Gopal Ch. 4 · Section 4.5

Closest Pair of Points

Given n points in 2D, find the pair with minimum Euclidean distance. Brute force: O(n²). D&C: O(n log n).
Complexity
Brute forceO(n²) — check all pairs
D&C algorithmO(n log n)
RecurrenceT(n) = 2T(n/2) + O(n)
PreprocessingSort by x and y: O(n log n)
Strip comparisonEach point vs at most 8 neighbors
Algorithm (Gopal Algorithm 20)
1. Presort
Sort points by x-coordinate (array X) and y-coordinate (array Y).
2. Divide
Split at median x. S₁ = left half, S₂ = right half.
3. Conquer
Recursively find closest pair (a,b) in S₁ and (c,d) in S₂. Let δ = min(dist(a,b), dist(c,d)).
4. Combine — The Strip
Check points within δ of the median line. Each point compared to at most 8 others in the strip.
The Strip Lemma (Gopal Lemma 4.12)
Key insight: In the strip of width 2δ around the median, each point needs to be compared with at most 8 other points.

Why? Consider a 2δ × 2δ square around any strip point u. Any two points in S₁ (or S₂) are at least δ apart. So any δ × δ sub-square holds at most 4 points, and the 2δ × 2δ square holds at most 16 (actually at most 8 can be closer than δ to u).

Since points in the strip are sorted by y-coordinate, we only compare each point to the next 8 in sorted order → O(n) total strip work → O(n log n) overall.
