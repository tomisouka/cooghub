1 — Balance Factor
Every node stores a balance factor = height(left) − height(right). If any node's balance factor leaves the range −1 to +1, the tree rotates to fix it.
Balance factor = height(left) − height(right). Every node here is balanced. If any node reaches ±2 after an insert or delete, a rotation fires.
2 — Triggering a Rotation
Insert 10 into the tree above. It goes left of 20. Now 30's balance factor becomes +2 — violation. Rotate right at 30.
After insert(10) — UNBALANCED
→
After right rotation at 30 — BALANCED
Right rotation at 30: 20 takes 30's place. 30 becomes 20's right child. 20's old right child (if any) becomes 30's left child. Balance restored.
3 — Four Rotation Cases
The shape of the imbalance determines which rotation to apply. Two are single rotations, two are double rotations.
Left Rotation
When: right-right heavy (bf = −2, right child bf ≤ 0)
Right Rotation
When: left-left heavy (bf = +2, left child bf ≥ 0)
Left-Right Rotation
When: left-right heavy (bf = +2, left child bf = −1). Rotate left child left first, then rotate root right.
Right-Left Rotation
When: right-left heavy (bf = −2, right child bf = +1). Rotate right child right first, then rotate root left.
