Special Limits
Squeeze Theoremg≤f≤h and lim g = lim h = L → lim f = L
Fundamental trig limitslim_{x→0} sinx/x = 1 lim_{x→0} (1−cosx)/x = 0
e definitionlim_{n→∞} (1+1/n)ⁿ = e
L'Hôpital's Rule — for 0/0 or ∞/∞ indeterminate forms
If lim f/g is 0/0 or ∞/∞ → lim f(x)/g(x) = lim f'(x)/g'(x)

Other forms: 0·∞, ∞−∞, 0⁰, 1^∞, ∞⁰ — rewrite first, then apply.
