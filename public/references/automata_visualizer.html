Pumping Lemma for Regular Languages

If L is regular, ∃ pumping length p such that every s ∈ L with |s| ≥ p splits as s = xyz where:
  ① |y| ≥ 1   ② |xy| ≤ p   ③ ∀i ≥ 0, xyⁱz ∈ L
To prove L not regular: assume it is, choose s, show every valid split fails ③.
